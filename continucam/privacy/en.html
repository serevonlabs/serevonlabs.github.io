Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Serevon Labs handles information when you use ContinuCam. ContinuCam is designed as a privacy-focused, on-device video recorder. It does not require a Serevon Labs account and does not upload your recordings to a Serevon Labs server.

1. Information ContinuCam accesses

ContinuCam may access information needed to provide features you choose to use, including camera and microphone input; camera/lens capabilities; recording orientation; available device storage; battery and charging status; thermal status; app/recording state; Google Play subscription entitlement information; Google Play update eligibility/status; and preferences stored locally on your device such as language, date/time format, PIN-lock settings, biometric preference, and authorized review-access state.

ContinuCam does not request access to your precise location, contacts, SMS messages, call history, or advertising identifier for its normal operation. ContinuCam does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Recordings and encrypted vault

Video and audio are captured only when you intentionally start a recording. Completed recordings are stored in ContinuCam's app-private storage and encrypted using AES-256-GCM. Current recordings use ContinuCam's CCV4 envelope-encryption design: each recording receives a random AES-256 data key, and key material is protected using Android Keystore. Legacy ContinuCam vault formats remain readable for compatibility.

ContinuCam does not upload your recordings to Serevon Labs. Recordings remain on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

3. Exporting and sharing

If you choose Export, ContinuCam creates an unencrypted media copy outside the encrypted vault. If you choose Share, ContinuCam may create a temporary unencrypted app-private cache copy and grant the receiving app temporary access. Once a recording is exported, shared, copied, backed up, or received by another app or person, ContinuCam cannot control how that copy is stored, transmitted, or used.

4. PIN and biometric security

ContinuCam does not store your PIN as readable plaintext. A verifier derived from the PIN and a salt is stored locally. Biometric matching is performed by Android's system biometric APIs. ContinuCam does not receive or store fingerprint, face, or other biometric templates.

5. Google Play subscriptions and updates

ContinuCam Premium is offered through Google Play. Payment details are processed by Google Play; ContinuCam does not receive or store your full payment-card information. ContinuCam receives purchase/subscription metadata needed to determine whether Premium features should be available and may store a local entitlement state.

ContinuCam may also ask Google Play whether an app update is available and may use Google Play's in-app update flow. Google's handling of information is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies.

6. Permissions and device services

Depending on the features you use, ContinuCam may request camera, microphone, biometric, foreground-service, wake-lock, and Google Play billing capabilities. Android may display camera/microphone privacy indicators and foreground-service information while recording. You can manage supported permissions in Android settings, but disabling a required permission may prevent the related feature from working.

7. Data sharing and sale

Serevon Labs does not sell your recordings or personal information and does not use ContinuCam to serve behavioral advertising. ContinuCam does not send recordings to advertisers, analytics providers, or Serevon Labs. Information may be handled by Google Play when you use billing or update services, by an app you explicitly choose when sharing a recording, or by your email provider if you contact customer support.

8. Retention and deletion

Encrypted recordings remain on your device until you delete them, clear ContinuCam app data, or uninstall the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling ContinuCam can also remove Android Keystore material needed to decrypt the private vault, making remaining encrypted files inaccessible. Exported copies are outside the vault and must be deleted separately from the location or app where you saved them.

Local preferences and entitlement caches generally remain until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

9. Security

ContinuCam uses app-private storage, authenticated encryption, Android Keystore protection, optional PIN/biometric access controls, and controlled export/share workflows to reduce unauthorized access. No software, device, or storage medium can be guaranteed completely secure, and Serevon Labs cannot guarantee that recordings will never be lost or accessed due to device compromise, operating-system failure, hardware failure, user action, or circumstances outside the app's control.

10. Children

ContinuCam is not designed to create a child account or knowingly collect personal information from children through a Serevon Labs service. Because recording laws and consent requirements vary, a parent, guardian, or other responsible adult should supervise use where required by law.

11. International use

ContinuCam's encrypted recordings are designed to remain on the device. Third-party services you choose to use, including Google Play, email, cloud backup, export destinations, or sharing apps, may process information in other countries under their own policies and applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when ContinuCam's features, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy changes.

13. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to Serevon Labs at serevon.studio@gmail.com.
